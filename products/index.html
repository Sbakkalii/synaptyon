§ 01 // The chain

Five stages. Seventeen products.

Documents move left to right: in through capture, read, understood, put to work, and remembered, with every step measured. Products share one state, so each stage builds on what the others found.

{% case c.id %}{% when "ingest" %}Everything starts with a usable page. These products take documents in from wherever they arrive, read every line, printed or handwritten, and rebuild the structure of the page.{% when "understand" %}Once a page is read, these products decide what it is, pull out the fields and entities a workflow needs, and check that the document can be trusted.{% when "apply" %}This is where documents do work: answers that cite their source, search by meaning, summaries and domain translation.{% when "remember" %}The layer that makes the chain improve with use: what was read is kept, checked before it is written, versioned, traceable and deletable.
